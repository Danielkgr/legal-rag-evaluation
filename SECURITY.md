# Security Policy

Legal RAG Evaluation is a working prototype that ships no documents, so you supply your own PDFs.  This policy covers the code in the repository and the documents you index with it.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report a security problem by email to Daniel Glynn-Roe at daniel.glynnroe@gmail.com instead of opening a public issue.  A public issue shows the problem to everyone, and that includes anyone who might use it against you, so it is best kept quiet until the problem is fixed.

Say as much as you can in that email.  Include what happened, the steps to repeat it, and the version or commit you were using.  If your report contains a proof of concept that could spread on its own, describe what it does without attaching it, and share the working copy once we confirm we understand it.

## What to expect

We reply within 14 days.  We confirm we have received your report, and we tell you when we think the problem is fixed.  We name you in the fix notes unless you ask to stay anonymous, and we never ask you to sign a non-disclosure agreement to report a problem here.  This project offers no bug bounty, and the note that credits your help is the reward we can give.

## Public disclosure

We will not publish the details of a reported problem until it is fixed, and we ask that anyone who reports one keep it private until then.  Once a fix is released we disclose what was wrong and who reported it, unless the reporter prefers otherwise.
